ALL ABOUT SPACE

Space is the huge, empty place that starts about 100 kilometers above Earth. It has almost no air, no sound, and is very cold. It holds every star, planet, and galaxy in the universe

What is in space?

  • Stars: Big balls of hot gas that make light and heat.
  • Planets: Round worlds that spin around stars.
  • Galaxies: Huge groups of billions of stars held together by gravity.
  • Black Holes: Super-dense spots with gravity so strong that not even light can escape.
  • Asteroids and Comets: Small leftover chunks of rock, metal, and ice.

Fast Facts about Space

  • Temperature: Space is extremely cold, sitting at about −270 °C (−455 °F).
  • No Sound: Sound needs air to move. Because space is a vacuum, it is totally silent.
  • Age: The universe is about 13.8 billion years old.
  • Our Solar System: Features eight planets orbiting one star, the Sun.
