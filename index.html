THE MAGICAL TRENCH

Pip was a tiny, puffy cloud no bigger than a kitten. He lived in a vast, purple evening sky. Every night, the moon would sing a low, humming lullaby. The big storm clouds grumbled and dropped heavy, cooling dew on the grass below. But Pip stayed light. He loved to bounce from one twinkling star to the next like a marshmallow on a trampoline.One night, the wind grew very soft. It whispered, “Shh, little Pip. It is time to rest.”Pip felt a gentle heavy warmth inside his fluffy white center. He floated down, past the tall pine trees, and hovered just above a quiet, glassy lake. The water acted like a big, silver mirror. Pip saw his own sleepy reflection looking back up at him. He curled his edges inward. He made himself into a soft, cozy pillow of mist right above the reeds.
